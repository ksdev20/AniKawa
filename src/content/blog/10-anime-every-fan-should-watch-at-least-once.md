# What Makes An Anime Worth Watching At Least Once

Every anime fan eventually asks the same question:

"What are the anime everyone should experience at least once?"

It's a surprisingly difficult question to answer.

The highest-rated anime aren't always the best recommendations.

The most popular anime aren't always the most memorable.

And some of the most influential anime ever made can feel difficult to recommend to modern viewers without important context.

So what actually makes an anime worth watching at least once?

For us, it comes down to impact.

The anime that stay with people long after the final episode are usually the ones that did something special.

Maybe they told an unforgettable story.

Maybe they introduced a world unlike anything you've seen before.

Maybe they made you think differently about a character, a theme, or even life itself.

The best anime create memories.

Years later, fans still remember certain scenes, certain conversations, and certain emotional moments.

Not because they're constantly reminded of them, but because those moments genuinely mattered.

Another important factor is representation.

Anime is an incredibly diverse medium.

It can tell action-packed adventures, intimate dramas, psychological thrillers, emotional coming-of-age stories, science-fiction epics, and everything in between.

A true must-watch list shouldn't focus on only one type of experience.

Instead, it should showcase the range of what anime can accomplish.

That's why this list isn't simply a ranking of the "best" anime.

It's a collection of anime that demonstrate different strengths of the medium.

Some are emotionally powerful.

Some are technically impressive.

Some changed the industry.

Others simply tell exceptional stories.

Not every anime here will become your personal favorite.

That's perfectly normal.

But each one offers something valuable that helps explain why anime has become such a beloved form of storytelling around the world.

# Why This List Isn't Just About Popularity

One of the biggest problems with anime recommendation lists is that popularity often becomes the only thing that matters.

The same handful of titles appear again and again.

Eventually, recommendations stop being recommendations and start becoming reflections of whatever happened to be the most popular at the time.

There's nothing wrong with popular anime.

Many of them earned their success.

But popularity alone doesn't guarantee a place on a must-watch list.

Likewise, some extraordinary anime never become mainstream hits despite offering experiences every fan should try at least once.

That's why this list takes a different approach.

Rather than asking which anime are the biggest, we asked which anime best represent the strengths of the medium itself.

We also considered accessibility.

A must-watch anime should still be enjoyable for modern audiences.

There are plenty of historically important series that feel dated today and require significant patience from new viewers.

Those anime still matter, but they aren't always the best recommendations for someone looking to experience anime's greatest strengths firsthand.

Another factor is completion.

Whenever possible, it's more satisfying to recommend anime that provide meaningful conclusions rather than leaving viewers with incomplete stories and unresolved plotlines.

Most importantly, we wanted variety.

If every recommendation falls into the same genre, the list fails its purpose.

Anime's greatest strength is its diversity.

The series below were chosen because each contributes something unique.

Together, they provide a broad look at why anime continues to attract passionate fans across generations.

# 10 Anime Every Fan Should Watch At Least Once

## 1. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

**Episodes:** 64

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

**Genres:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy

If there is one anime that consistently appears near the top of recommendation lists, it's Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood.

And unlike many overhyped classics, it largely earns that reputation.

The story follows brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric as they search for a way to restore their bodies after a disastrous attempt to use alchemy to bring their mother back to life.

What begins as a personal journey gradually expands into a massive story involving political conspiracies, philosophical questions, war, sacrifice, and redemption.

Few anime balance so many elements so effectively.

The action is exciting.

The worldbuilding is excellent.

The emotional moments land.

And perhaps most importantly, the story delivers a satisfying conclusion.

For many viewers, Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood represents the closest thing anime has to an all-purpose recommendation.

**Why You Should Watch It**

One of the most complete and consistently excellent anime ever made.

**Who Should Watch It**

Anyone looking for a fantasy adventure with strong storytelling.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its ability to excel in nearly every category.

**Potential Drawback**

The early episodes move quickly because they assume familiarity with the original adaptation.

---

## 2. Attack on Titan

**Episodes:** 94

**Seasons:** 4

**Status:** Complete

**Genres:** Action, Mystery, Drama

Very few anime have had the cultural impact of Attack on Titan.

When it first appeared, many viewers expected a straightforward survival story about humanity fighting giant monsters.

What they received instead was something far more ambitious.

Attack on Titan constantly evolves.

Every season changes your understanding of the world.

Every answer introduces new questions.

And every major revelation forces viewers to reconsider what they thought they knew.

Beyond its twists and mysteries, the anime excels at creating tension.

Few series generate anticipation and discussion as effectively.

Combined with memorable characters and enormous thematic ambition, it became one of the defining anime of its generation.

Whether you ultimately love every creative decision or not, it's impossible to deny its significance.

**Why You Should Watch It**

One of the most ambitious and talked-about anime ever created.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of action, mystery, and large-scale storytelling.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its ability to constantly reinvent itself.

**Potential Drawback**

The darker tone may not appeal to every viewer.

---

## 3. Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

**Episodes:** 28+

**Seasons:** 1+

**Status:** Ongoing

**Genres:** Fantasy, Adventure, Drama

Most fantasy stories focus on defeating the final villain.

Frieren begins after that story has already ended.

The anime follows Frieren, an elven mage who outlives the heroes she once traveled with and slowly begins to understand the value of the time they spent together.

It's a simple premise.

But the execution is extraordinary.

Rather than relying on constant battles or dramatic twists, Frieren finds meaning in small moments, quiet reflections, and personal connections.

The result is a fantasy anime that feels mature, thoughtful, and surprisingly emotional.

It reminds viewers that adventures aren't defined solely by great achievements.

They're also defined by the memories left behind.

Few modern anime have received as much universal praise while feeling this unique.

**Why You Should Watch It**

A fresh and deeply human approach to fantasy storytelling.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of character-driven adventures and emotional journeys.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its reflective tone and exceptional writing.

**Potential Drawback**

Its slower pace may not suit viewers seeking nonstop action.

---

## 4. Steins;Gate

**Episodes:** 24

**Seasons:** 1 Main Story

**Status:** Complete Main Story

**Genres:** Sci-Fi, Thriller

Steins;Gate is one of the most respected science-fiction anime ever made.

The story begins with a group of friends accidentally discovering a method of sending messages through time.

At first, the consequences seem harmless.

Eventually, they become anything but.

What makes Steins;Gate special is its structure.

The early episodes spend time building characters and relationships before gradually transforming into a tense, emotional thriller.

That patience pays off enormously later.

The science-fiction concepts remain engaging, but the real strength of the anime lies in its characters and emotional stakes.

By the end, it becomes clear why so many fans consider it one of the medium's greatest achievements.

**Why You Should Watch It**

A brilliant blend of science fiction and emotional storytelling.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of time travel, mysteries, and character-driven drama.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its rewarding long-term storytelling.

**Potential Drawback**

The slower opening may test some viewers' patience.

---

## 5. Mob Psycho 100

**Episodes:** 37

**Seasons:** 3

**Status:** Complete

**Genres:** Action, Comedy, Supernatural

Mob Psycho 100 could have been remembered solely for its incredible animation.

Instead, it became one of the best character-growth stories in anime.

The series follows Shigeo "Mob" Kageyama, an immensely powerful psychic who struggles not with villains, but with understanding himself and finding confidence as a person.

That distinction makes all the difference.

The battles are spectacular.

The comedy is consistently funny.

But the emotional core of the story is what truly elevates the series.

Mob's journey feels authentic.

His successes feel earned.

And the anime never loses sight of the human story beneath the supernatural powers.

By the final season, it's clear that Mob Psycho 100 is about far more than psychic battles.

It's about becoming the best version of yourself.

**Why You Should Watch It**

One of anime's greatest examples of character development.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of action with genuine emotional depth.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its perfect balance of spectacle and heart.

**Potential Drawback**

The art style may look unusual at first glance.

## 6. Violet Evergarden

**Episodes:** 13

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete Main Story + Movies

**Genres:** Drama, Slice of Life

Anime is often praised for its action, fantasy worlds, and creativity.

Violet Evergarden is a reminder that some of the medium's greatest strengths come from its ability to tell deeply human stories.

The series follows Violet, a former child soldier trying to understand emotions after the war that defined her life has ended.

As she begins writing letters for other people, she slowly learns what words like love, grief, regret, and hope truly mean.

What follows is one of the most emotionally powerful journeys in anime.

Every episode explores different lives and different struggles while gradually developing Violet herself.

The animation remains among the most beautiful ever produced for television anime, but visuals alone aren't what make this series special.

It's the empathy.

The humanity.

And the emotional honesty behind every story.

**Why You Should Watch It**

One of the most moving anime ever created.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of emotional storytelling and character-driven drama.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its breathtaking combination of visual beauty and emotional depth.

**Potential Drawback**

The slower pace may not appeal to viewers looking for constant excitement.

---

## 7. Death Note

**Episodes:** 37

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

**Genres:** Thriller, Mystery, Psychological

There are very few anime that have introduced more people to the medium than Death Note.

Its premise is instantly compelling.

A student discovers a supernatural notebook capable of killing anyone whose name is written inside it.

Rather than becoming a simple power fantasy, the story transforms into a tense psychological battle between Light Yagami and the legendary detective known as L.

The reason Death Note remains essential viewing years after its release is accessibility.

You don't need anime experience to enjoy it.

The story hooks viewers immediately.

The stakes are clear.

The characters are fascinating.

And the constant battle of intelligence keeps the tension high throughout.

It remains one of the easiest anime to recommend and one of the hardest to stop watching once you've started.

**Why You Should Watch It**

One of the greatest gateway anime ever made.

**Who Should Watch It**

Anyone interested in suspense, mystery, and psychological warfare.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its addictive cat-and-mouse storytelling.

**Potential Drawback**

Some viewers prefer the first half over the later episodes.

---

## 8. A Place Further Than the Universe

**Episodes:** 13

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

**Genres:** Adventure, Drama

Not every must-watch anime needs world-ending stakes or supernatural powers.

A Place Further Than the Universe proves that ordinary people pursuing extraordinary goals can be just as compelling.

The story follows four girls who decide to join an expedition to Antarctica.

On paper, it sounds simple.

In execution, it's one of the most inspiring anime ever made.

The series explores friendship, courage, ambition, and the importance of taking chances while you still can.

Its characters feel authentic.

Their struggles feel real.

And their journey becomes surprisingly emotional by the time it reaches its destination.

Few anime capture the feeling of adventure quite so effectively.

It's a celebration of youth, growth, and the experiences that shape us.

**Why You Should Watch It**

A genuinely uplifting and unforgettable journey.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of adventure, friendship, and personal growth stories.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its ability to inspire without becoming unrealistic.

**Potential Drawback**

Viewers seeking action-heavy stories may want something different.

---

## 9. Cowboy Bebop

**Episodes:** 26

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

**Genres:** Sci-Fi, Action

Some anime become classics because of nostalgia.

Cowboy Bebop became a classic because it genuinely holds up.

Released in the late 1990s, the series follows a group of bounty hunters traveling through space while carrying the weight of their pasts.

Each episode blends science fiction, action, jazz-inspired style, and surprisingly mature storytelling.

What makes Cowboy Bebop important isn't just its quality.

It's its influence.

The anime helped introduce countless international viewers to the medium and demonstrated that animated stories could appeal to adult audiences without sacrificing entertainment.

Even today, its direction, soundtrack, atmosphere, and character writing remain exceptional.

Few anime feel as effortlessly cool.

And even fewer remain this relevant decades after release.

**Why You Should Watch It**

One of the most influential anime ever created.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of science fiction, stylish storytelling, and memorable characters.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its timeless atmosphere and incredible soundtrack.

**Potential Drawback**

Its episodic structure may feel different from modern serialized anime.

---

## 10. March Comes in Like a Lion

**Episodes:** 44

**Seasons:** 2

**Status:** Complete Adapted Arc

**Genres:** Drama, Slice of Life

If there is one anime on this list that deserves more attention from newer viewers, it's March Comes in Like a Lion.

The story follows Rei Kiriyama, a young professional shogi player struggling with loneliness, depression, and finding his place in the world.

What makes the anime remarkable is its understanding of people.

Few series portray emotional struggles with such empathy and nuance.

The writing is thoughtful.

The characters feel real.

And every relationship contributes meaningfully to Rei's growth.

Rather than relying on dramatic twists or major action sequences, the anime finds power in everyday moments.

Small victories.

Meaningful conversations.

Gradual healing.

It's one of the most human stories anime has ever told.

**Why You Should Watch It**

A masterpiece of character writing and emotional storytelling.

**Who Should Watch It**

Anyone who enjoys thoughtful, character-focused drama.

**What Makes It Stand Out**

Its deeply empathetic portrayal of personal growth.

**Potential Drawback**

Its slower pace requires patience.

# Why These 10

Whenever a list like this appears, people immediately ask the same question:

"Why wasn't my favorite anime included?"

The answer is simple.

This article isn't trying to identify the ten greatest anime ever made.

It's trying to showcase the range of what anime can accomplish.

That's why you'll notice some major names are missing.

Not because they're bad.

Far from it.

Series like Naruto, One Piece, Bleach, and many other popular titles have earned millions of fans worldwide.

But this list prioritizes variety over popularity.

Each recommendation highlights a different strength of the medium.

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood demonstrates balanced storytelling.

Attack on Titan showcases ambition and scale.

Frieren represents modern fantasy at its best.

Steins;Gate highlights science fiction.

Violet Evergarden focuses on emotion.

Cowboy Bebop represents anime history and influence.

Together, these series provide a broader understanding of anime than simply choosing the ten most famous titles ever made.

# Final Thoughts

No single article can fully capture everything anime has to offer.

The medium is simply too large, too diverse, and too creative for any list to be truly complete.

But if someone asked for ten anime that demonstrate why fans around the world love this medium so much, these would be difficult to ignore.

Some will make you think.

Some will make you laugh.

Some will make you cry.

And some will completely change your expectations of what animated storytelling can achieve.

Most importantly, every anime on this list offers an experience worth remembering.

That's what separates a good anime from one every fan should watch at least once.

Not perfection.

Not popularity.

Not ratings.

Impact.

And these ten anime have left a lasting impact on countless viewers for a reason.
