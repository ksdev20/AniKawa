# Why Starting Anime Can Feel Overwhelming

Anime is more popular than ever, but that's exactly what makes it difficult for newcomers. Search for anime recommendations and you'll immediately find thousands of titles, endless rankings, and long-running series with hundreds of episodes.

The good news is that you don't need to start with massive franchises. Many of the best beginner anime are relatively short, easy to understand, and showcase what makes anime such a unique form of entertainment.

This guide focuses on series that are accessible, engaging, and beginner-friendly from the very first episode.

# What Makes an Anime Beginner-Friendly

For this list, we prioritized anime that have strong opening episodes, clear storytelling, manageable episode counts, and little to no watch-order confusion.

Most importantly, these are shows that can hook someone who has never watched anime before.

# 10 Best Animes Online for Beginners

## 1. Death Note

**Episodes:** 37

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

A brilliant student discovers a supernatural notebook capable of killing anyone whose name is written inside it. What follows is one of anime's greatest battles of intelligence.

**Why You Should Watch It**

The premise is immediately interesting and the story rarely slows down.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of mystery, thrillers, crime dramas, and psychological mind games.

**Potential Drawback**

Some viewers prefer the first half of the series over the second.

---

## 2. Spy × Family

**Episodes:** 37

**Seasons:** 2

**Status:** Ongoing

A spy, an assassin, and a telepath unknowingly become a fake family while each hides a major secret.

**Why You Should Watch It**

It's funny, wholesome, and easy to enjoy even if you've never watched anime before.

**Who Should Watch It**

Anyone looking for comedy, action, and family-friendly entertainment.

---

## 3. Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

**Episodes:** 28

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Ongoing

Instead of telling the story of a hero's journey, Frieren explores what happens after the adventure ends.

**Why You Should Watch It**

It offers a refreshing fantasy experience filled with emotion and beautiful world-building.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fantasy fans and viewers who enjoy slower, character-focused stories.

**Potential Drawback**

The pacing is intentionally relaxed.

---

## 4. Mob Psycho 100

**Episodes:** 37

**Seasons:** 3

**Status:** Complete

Mob may be one of the strongest psychics alive, but learning how to grow as a person is his real challenge.

**Why You Should Watch It**

Fantastic action scenes combined with genuine character development.

**Who Should Watch It**

Viewers looking for both action and emotional depth.

---

## 5. One Punch Man

**Episodes:** 24

**Seasons:** 2

**Status:** Ongoing

Saitama can defeat any opponent with a single punch, leaving him desperately searching for a challenge.

**Why You Should Watch It**

Fast-paced, hilarious, and packed with memorable action.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of superhero stories and comedy.

---

## 6. Your Lie in April

**Episodes:** 22

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

A talented pianist rediscovers music after meeting an energetic violinist.

**Why You Should Watch It**

An emotional story with unforgettable music and characters.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of drama, romance, and emotional storytelling.

---

## 7. Erased

**Episodes:** 12

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

A struggling manga artist is sent back in time to prevent a tragic crime from happening.

**Why You Should Watch It**

A gripping mystery that's easy to binge over a weekend.

**Who Should Watch It**

Thriller and suspense fans.

---

## 8. Violet Evergarden

**Episodes:** 13

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

A former soldier searches for meaning and human connection after the war ends.

**Why You Should Watch It**

Some of the most beautiful animation ever produced.

**Who Should Watch It**

Viewers who enjoy emotional and character-driven stories.

---

## 9. Odd Taxi

**Episodes:** 13

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

A taxi driver becomes involved in a strange mystery connecting criminals, celebrities, and missing persons.

**Why You Should Watch It**

Excellent writing and a mystery that rewards attention.

**Who Should Watch It**

Fans of crime dramas and suspense.

---

## 10. A Place Further than the Universe

**Episodes:** 13

**Seasons:** 1

**Status:** Complete

Four girls embark on a life-changing expedition to Antarctica.

**Why You Should Watch It**

Inspiring, uplifting, and surprisingly emotional.

**Who Should Watch It**

Anyone looking for adventure, friendship, and personal growth.

# Which Anime Should You Start With

**Best Overall First Anime:** Death Note

**Best Family-Friendly Choice:** Spy × Family

**Best Fantasy Anime:** Frieren

**Best Action Series:** Mob Psycho 100

**Best Comedy:** One Punch Man

**Best Emotional Story:** Your Lie in April

**Best Mystery:** Erased

**Best Visual Experience:** Violet Evergarden

**Best Crime Thriller:** Odd Taxi

**Best Adventure Story:** A Place Further than the Universe

# Final Thoughts

Starting anime doesn't have to be overwhelming. The best first anime is simply the one that sounds the most interesting to you.

Whether you're looking for suspense, fantasy, comedy, romance, or adventure, the series on this list offer excellent entry points into the world of anime. Pick one, watch a few episodes, and discover what makes anime so special.
