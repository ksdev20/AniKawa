# What Makes An Anime Underrated

Some anime never receive the popularity they deserve despite offering excellent storytelling, memorable characters, and unique ideas.

# Top Underrated Anime

## Odd Taxi

A mystery masterpiece with outstanding writing and one of anime's most satisfying endings.

## Vivy: Fluorite Eye's Song

A visually stunning sci-fi anime exploring artificial intelligence and humanity.

## Sonny Boy

An experimental anime that rewards viewers willing to embrace mystery and symbolism.

## Acca: 13-Territory Inspection Dept.

A political drama featuring excellent world-building and intrigue.

## Astra Lost in Space

A thrilling space adventure filled with twists and engaging character development.

# Final Thoughts

If you're tired of mainstream recommendations, these hidden gems offer some of the most rewarding anime experiences available today.
